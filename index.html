Privacy Policy

Effective date & last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DALIL SURIYA (Arabic: دليل سوريا; “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our Android application (“the App”) and our website https://ameryruw.github.io/Dalil-fi-Syria/ (“the Website”). By using the App or the Website you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the service.

1. Who we are

DALIL SURIYA is a public, community-driven business directory for the cities of Syria. The service is operated by our team on behalf of the agent responsible for the directory, Mr. Ammar Al-Mustafa Al-Hasan (Raqqa, Syria). For any privacy question or request you can reach us at any time through the contact channels listed in section 13 — we answer in Arabic, Russian, Kurdish, English, German and Turkish.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you submit voluntarily. When you apply to add a business, workshop or service to the directory, we collect the details you enter in the application form: business name, category and subcategory, city, address, working hours, phone number and/or WhatsApp number, a short description, optional map coordinates and an optional photo. Applications are reviewed by our administrator before publication; only approved listings become publicly visible. When you rate a business we store the star rating you submit. When you use the “Report” feature we store the reason and any optional note you attach — reports are visible to the administrator only, not to other users.

2.2 Location information. We collect your location only when you actively use the “Near me” feature or the map distance tools. In that case your device’s operating system or browser asks for your permission first, and your precise or approximate location is processed on your device only to sort nearby results — it is never stored, logged or transmitted to our servers. You can always enter your coordinates manually instead. When you open the map, your device also downloads map imagery from third-party tile servers (see section 5); those requests necessarily reveal your IP address and the map area you are viewing to those providers.

2.3 Information stored on your device only. Using browser local storage we keep: your language and theme preferences, your favourites list, cached content for offline use, and small anti-abuse markers (for example, to prevent duplicate ratings and spam submissions). This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us as part of your profile, and is removed when you clear the browser/app storage or uninstall the App.

2.4 What we do not collect. We do not run advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs, we do not collect advertising identifiers for marketing, and we do not process payments inside the App — payments, where applicable, are arranged directly with our agent outside the App, and no payment-card data ever reaches us through the service.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to: operate and display the directory; review, approve and moderate listings, ratings and reports; communicate with you about your application using the contact details you provided; keep the service safe (anti-spam, rate limiting, duplicate detection, abuse prevention); and comply with applicable law. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for behavioural advertising or profiling.

4. Public nature of listings

The directory is public by design: approved listings — including the business name, address, working hours and the phone/WhatsApp number you choose to publish — and the star ratings of a business are visible to all visitors of the App and the Website. Do not submit information you are not authorised to publish or that you do not want to be public. You may ask us to correct or remove a listing at any time (section 8).

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase / Firestore (Google LLC) — cloud database that stores listings, ratings and reports. Data is processed under Google’s Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and may include automatically generated identifiers used for security and abuse prevention.
  • Map imagery providers — map tiles are loaded from Google Maps, Esri (ArcGIS Online) and the OpenStreetMap Foundation when you open the map; each provider processes requests under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) — hosting infrastructure for the Website.

6. App permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions: Internet (required — to sync directory data and load map tiles) and precise / approximate location (optional — requested at the moment you use “Near me”, never in the background). Denying the location permission does not limit any other part of the service.

7. Data retention

Listings are retained while their subscription is active and may be unpublished after it expires; they are deleted upon a verified removal request. Ratings and reports are retained as long as the related listing exists, or until deletion is requested. Data stored on your device (section 2.3) is kept until you clear it or uninstall the App. We may retain minimal records where necessary to prevent repeat abuse or where required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including, for users in the EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation), you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; have inaccurate data corrected; have your data erased (“right to be forgotten”); restrict or object to processing; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent — for example, you can simply deny the location permission at any time in your device settings. To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +963 939 481 984 or use the “Report” feature in the App. We respond within a reasonable time after verifying your request.

9. Children’s privacy

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, stored in Google Firebase with security rules that restrict writes, and moderated by an administrator whose account is protected by email/password authentication. We also apply anti-spam and rate-limiting measures. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International data transfers

Our cloud database is operated by Google Firebase, so your submitted data may be stored and processed in the United States or other regions where Google and its service providers operate. By submitting information you understand and accept such processing in accordance with this Policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top will always show the current version, and material changes will be highlighted inside the App. Continued use of the App or Website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
